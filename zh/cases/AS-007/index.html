Albert Bierstadt · Sublime Landscapes and Dusk

Albert Bierstadt

比尔施塔特把山谷、湖岸和草原组织成宏阔而富于情绪的空间。深色树群与岩壁围住远处的亮天、薄雾和水面；暖金受光区在蓝灰风暴云、暗绿树林与褐紫阴影间展开。近景的细节与远景的柔和过渡共同制造可走入的深度。本条以六幅已对应作品的用户复现图归纳风格，另两张同批附图暂存于待核实区。

怎样看这组作品

19 世纪美国浪漫主义风景 · 哈德逊河画派背景 · 戏剧性明暗、空气透视与发光的天水

先看大块深影与局部亮光如何引导视线，再看近景枝叶、谷底薄雾与远山怎样分层。宏阔感来自光域、遮挡、空气透视和小尺度生命；山景的风暴光与湖岸、草原的暮色是同一套光影语言的不同变化。

阿尔伯特·比尔施塔特（Albert Bierstadt，1830–1902）为德裔美国画家。作品题名、年代、媒介与馆藏据各图来源，风格特征按可见画面归纳。六张主要参考为用户提供的作品复现图；不同复制版本可能有色彩与边缘差异，不能据此复原原作的精确色值或制作流程。中文题名为图鉴译名。全部原文件、可见署名与复现品水印保留；“油画世界 ArtYouhua”是所见复现品水印，不视作原作者签名。附图 7、8 归属及题名待核实，未参与本条提示词归纳。

完整品鉴笔记

光域与深影

大面积低亮度岩壁、云底和树群托起局部金黄或乳白受光区。光常从云隙或谷口进入，再由雾、水面和岩坡承接；最亮处成为视线的落点。

空间与尺度

前景以树干、岩块、草坡和遮挡建立入口，中景展开放光的湖水、草地或溪流，远景逐层淡入山体与云气。鹿、野牛和微小人物让自然显得辽阔。

云雾与色温

云层有清晰的体积转折，边缘由暖亮渐变到冷灰蓝和褐紫。远山较浅、较柔和，薄雾降低远处对比；夕照中的金橙色与深绿、冷灰暗部相互支撑。

细节的疏密

近景树皮、叶簇、岩石和草地具有细致但不均匀的变化，强轮廓集中在近处或剪影。远景轮廓与纹理逐渐消隐，避免把所有距离画得同样清楚。

树形与水面

直立松树与伸展树冠作为深色框景，枝条和倒木带出横向或斜向节奏。平静水面保留亮天、山壁和树木的倒影，局部雾带或细小波纹打断镜像。

系列的变化

附图 1、2 展现风暴与穿云光；附图 3、5、6 偏重山谷、湖泊的金色暮光；附图 4 以野牛、溪流和树冠组织草原景色。宏阔感可以通过不同题材实现，并不需要重复同一种山峰。

复用时的取舍

保留输入图的空间骨架，用大块深影、受光焦点和远近对比建立光影秩序，再重绘细节。开阔景色可强化空气透视；树林或城市场景可借助原有树群、建筑和道路框景；人物照片保持身份与姿态，避免让背景喧宾夺主。

需要避开的倾向

避免把每张照片都改成美国西部山谷、强行添加雪峰或鹿群；避免全画橙黄、纯黑暗部、同等清晰的远近细节、夸张光束和只有调色没有绘画重构的处理。

城市云景 → 浪漫主义光影

保留原照片的宽幅视角、低位城市天际线、近景建筑关系与左侧小飞机。将厚云完整改绘为冷蓝灰与褐紫的体积，原有亮云区域转为金色和乳白光域；近景建筑以较丰富笔触重绘，远处轮廓通过薄雾与较柔和边缘后退。

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: complete panoramic painting, AI reinterpretation of a user photograph.
Input images: Image 1 is the sole EDIT TARGET and sole authority for content, composition, architecture, objects, viewpoint and aspect ratio. Image 2 (Bierstadt storm landscape) and Image 3 (Bierstadt Yosemite valley) are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: borrow their broad light-and-shadow structure, painted cloud volumes, luminous highlights, subtle colored shadows and atmospheric depth. Do not reproduce their terrain, animals, vegetation, water, signatures, text or watermarks.

Primary request:
Repaint the entire input image using the Romantic visual language of Albert Bierstadt’s landscapes: expansive space, dramatic light and shadow, luminous warm highlights, soft atmospheric perspective and richly observed foreground detail. Let the input determine content, composition and viewpoint; use the references for style. Preserve subject count, recognizable identities and poses, object relationships, existing terrain or architecture, aspect ratio and camera. Establish broad value masses first. Use existing trees, cliffs, buildings or foreground forms as layered dark framing, and organize a clear warm golden or ivory focal light within the scene’s existing illuminated areas. Keep a plausible light direction. Build shadows from deep green, brown-violet and cool blue-gray, retaining subtle internal variation. Only where the input already contains sky, paint volumetric clouds, thin haze, soft dusk light or restrained shafts of sunlight; do not invent sky or force a change of weather or season. Strengthen depth through overlap, progressively lower contrast, cooler and lighter distance and softer distant edges. Repaint existing foliage, rocks, fabric and surfaces with varied visible painted detail, richer near the viewer and simpler farther away, subordinating detail to light. Only where water already exists, paint reflections consistent with the original scene, selectively interrupted by small ripples or thin haze. Keep faces recognizable, poses credible and rendering consistent throughout. Convey grandeur, quiet or an approaching change of weather through light and space. Do not import snow peaks, giant cliffs, pine forests, waterfalls, bison, deer, sailboats or extra people from the references, or relocate the scene. Avoid uniformly orange color, featureless black silhouettes, equally sharp detail at every distance, excessive light beams, plastic materials, uniform texture, photographic patches, fabricated signatures, lettering and new watermarks.

Image-specific invariants:
This is a modern city panorama viewed from above nearby pale stone apartment rooftops. Keep the original very wide 20:9 aspect ratio (4080:1836), full frame, camera and perspective. The dense city and recognizable distant skyline remain low in the composition, approximately the bottom fifth, with the existing tall tower toward the right, the slimmer spire farther left and the rectangular tall building to its right all retained in their original locations and relative scales. Preserve the small single airplane just above the left-side rooftop at its existing position, scale and orientation. Preserve both large left and right foreground apartment blocks, their cornices, windows, rooftop railings, stairways and all intervening buildings. There are no visible people, no mountains, no vegetation foreground and no water to add.
Keep the layered overcast cloud arrangement and broad dark cloud canopy occupying the upper frame. Let warm ivory and restrained pale gold subtly illuminate the existing brighter cloud opening on the left and upper-middle, while the upper-right cloud mass stays cool blue-gray and brown-violet. Do not add a visible sun, change to a blazing sunset, exaggerate shafts of light or erase the existing cloudy weather. Align subtle warm rooftop accents with this soft light from the left/upper-left.
Repaint every part with coherent fine oil-painting-like marks: substantial but softly transitioned cloud forms; layered cool atmospheric haze and softer edges across the distant skyline; richer individually varied painted stone, windows and roof details in the near buildings. Do not preserve photographic patches or merely apply a color filter. Keep all forms legible and consistent, with the grand cloud field carrying the emotional scale. No border, no lettering, no added signature or watermark.

用途：风格迁移。
素材类型：完整的全景绘画，对用户照片进行 AI 改绘。
输入图片：图 1 是唯一的编辑目标，也是内容、构图、建筑、物件、视点和画幅比例的唯一依据。图 2（比尔施塔特的风暴风景）和图 3（比尔施塔特的优胜美地山谷）仅作风格参考：借用其大块明暗结构、绘画化云层体积、发光亮部、微妙的彩色阴影和空气层次。不得复制参考中的地形、动物、植被、水面、署名、文字或水印。

主要要求：
将整张输入图片改绘为 Albert Bierstadt 风景画的浪漫主义视觉语言：开阔空间、戏剧性明暗、发光的暖色亮部、柔和的空气透视，以及观察细致的近景细节。内容、构图和视点由输入图决定，参考仅提供风格。保留主体数量、可辨认的身份与姿态、物件关系、现有地形或建筑、画幅比例和镜头。先建立大块明暗。以现有树木、峭壁、建筑或近景形体作为分层的深色框景，在场景已有的受光区组织清楚的暖金色或象牙白焦点光域。保持合理的光线方向。用深绿、褐紫和冷蓝灰构建阴影，保留其中微妙的变化。仅在输入图本来有天空时，绘制有体积的云、薄雾、柔和暮光或克制的日光束；不要凭空增加天空，也不要强行改变天气或季节。通过遮挡、逐步降低对比、更冷更浅的远景以及更柔和的远处边缘，加强深度。以丰富且有变化的可见绘画细节重绘现有枝叶、岩石、织物和表面，近处丰富、远处简练，让细节服从光线。仅在原本有水时，绘制符合原场景的倒影，并以细小波纹或薄雾选择性地打断。保持面部可辨认、姿态可信，全画绘制方式一致。通过光线和空间传达宏阔、安静或天气即将变化的感受。不要从参考中加入雪峰、巨崖、松林、瀑布、野牛、鹿、帆船或额外人物，也不要改变场景地点。避免全画统一橙色、毫无层次的黑色剪影、所有距离同样锐利的细节、过强光束、塑料材质、均匀纹理、摄影贴片、虚构签名、文字和新增水印。

本图必须保持的内容：
这是一幅从近处浅色石材公寓楼屋顶上方观看的现代城市全景。保留原图非常宽的 20:9 画幅比例（4080:1836）、完整画面、镜头与透视。密集城区和可辨认的远方天际线仍应位于构图低处，约占底部五分之一；靠右的现有高塔、更左侧较细的尖塔以及尖塔右边的矩形高楼，都保留原位置与相对尺度。保留左侧屋顶正上方那架小型单架飞机的位置、尺度与朝向。保留左右两座大型近景公寓楼，包括檐口、窗户、屋顶栏杆、楼梯和所有中间建筑。画面中没有可见人物、山脉或近景植被，也没有需要添加的水面。
保留层叠阴云的布局，以及覆盖画面上部的大片暗云层。让暖象牙白与克制的浅金色轻柔照亮左侧和中上部现有的较亮云隙，右上方云团保持冷蓝灰与褐紫。不要添加可见太阳，不要改为炽烈日落，不要夸大光束，也不要抹掉原有多云天气。屋顶细微的暖色强调应与来自左侧／左上方的柔光一致。
