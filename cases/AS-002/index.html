Complete observation notes

Contours and lines

Color organization

Cobalt blue sky and richly varied greens establish the main color relationship, while ivory flowers and gold lines suggest daylight.

Surface texture

Dense yet distinct repetitions of plants, tiny flowers and brushwork on a paper-like surface create a handmade appearance reminiscent of wallpaper or tapestry.

Spatial composition

The foreground leaves resemble flat ornament, while the woodland, stream and hillside retain depth; detail is concentrated in the vegetation and contours.

Narrative atmosphere

A fresh, peaceful, idealized pastoral world. When transferring the style, prioritize preserving the original scene; there is no need to add streams or mushrooms.

Choices when reusing these features

Use gold lines to reorganize existing plants, clouds and architectural edges, retaining the bright space and visually open areas.

Tendencies to avoid

Avoid covering the entire sky with decorative lines or filling every area with the same density of detail, making the original scene hard to recognize.

中文

将输入照片完整改绘为新艺术与工艺美术气质融合的装饰性田园插画。照片决定内容、视角与构图；附加参考图仅提供风格语言。保留主体数量与身份、主要物件位置、建筑或地形结构、画幅比例。使用优雅的有机曲线、细金色轮廓与叶脉、密集而有节奏的植物纹样。以钴蓝、森林绿、翠绿、象牙白和赭金构成明亮配色，用柔和日光形成清新宁静的童话田园气氛。在原图已有植物、云层、织物或装饰表面加入图案，保留主体附近的视觉留白与空间纵深。整体呈精细手绘与轻微纸面颜料质感。不要导入参考中的溪流、山丘、蘑菇或新人物，不要添加文字、水印或边框，不要只叠加色彩滤镜。

English

Fully reinterpret the input photograph as a decorative pastoral illustration combining Art Nouveau and Arts and Crafts qualities. The photograph determines content, viewpoint and composition; additional reference images provide stylistic language only. Preserve subject count and identities, the positions of main objects, architectural or terrain structures, and aspect ratio. Use graceful organic curves, fine gold contours and leaf veins, and dense yet rhythmic botanical patterns. Build a bright palette of cobalt blue, forest green, emerald green, ivory and ochre-gold, using soft daylight to create a fresh, peaceful, fairy-tale pastoral atmosphere. Add patterns to plants, clouds, textiles or decorative surfaces already in the original image, retaining visually open areas around the main subject and spatial depth. Give the whole image a finely hand-painted appearance with subtle pigment-on-paper texture. Do not import streams, hills, mushrooms or new people from the reference. Do not add text, watermarks or borders, or merely overlay a color filter.
