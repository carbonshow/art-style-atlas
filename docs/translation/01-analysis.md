# Translation analysis

The site teaches beginners to observe visual characteristics and select transferable features. Its content distinguishes broad artistic traditions, specific visual examples, verified works, unverified attachments and actual historical AI reinterpretation records.

The main risks are overstating attribution/material certainty, omitting negative constraints in prompt translations, treating a descriptive case title as an official work title, and inconsistent names across navigation, detail pages and search.

Approved style: professional and accessible, with equivalent information in both languages. Translation is reviewed against the source rather than judged by back-translation alone. Shared terms and instructions are in 02-prompt.md; glossary.md records source-backed terminology and context restrictions.
